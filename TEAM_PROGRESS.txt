------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PROJECT IS (in simple words)
------------------------------------------------------------
We predict heart disease (CAD) + 3 vessel risks (LAD/LCX/RCA)
from routine clinical data, show it on interactive 3D heart,
and explain why with SHAP. Web app + disclaimer.
